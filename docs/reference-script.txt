WHY THESE LINES

E1/A1  Plain prose. Baseline naturalness, nothing to hide behind.
E2/A2  Currency grouping (R1 250,00) and a decimal comma. Engines either
       say "one thousand two hundred and fifty rand" or read it as a list.
E3/A3  Date + time. Common failure: "four November" vs "the fourth of".
E4/A4  South African proper nouns, including a hyphenated one (e-toll/e-tol).
E5/A5  Acronyms. SAA and NPO are spoken as words or spelled out; VAT/BTW
       differs between the two languages. Wrong choice is instantly jarring.
E6/A6  Long subordinate clause ending in a question. This is the line that
       separates a real engine from a flat one: prosody, not phonemes.
