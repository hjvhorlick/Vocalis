# DATASETS AND RIGHTS — VOCALIS
## Version 2.0
**Last Updated:** 2026-11-04

### 22. Training Data Requirements (Directive Part I, Section 22)
**Mandatory:** Documented rights for all training data.
**Prohibited:** Unlicensed pretrained speech models, external commercial TTS data, Piper-derived data, copied code.

### Allowed Sources
1. **Properly licensed datasets** (commercial usage rights verified)
2. **Commissioned recordings** (explicit commercial training rights, speaker consent)
3. **Synthetic material** (generated by Vocalis for experimental purposes)

### Record Keeping (Required for each dataset)
- **Dataset ID:** unique identifier
- **Dataset Name:** descriptive name
- **Source Type:** licensed / commissioned / synthetic
- **Speaker Consent:** documented (for commissioned/real recordings)
- **Dataset Licence:** exact licence text/reference
- **Commercial Usage Rights:** confirmed (yes/no/conditional)
- **Training Permissions:** documented scope
- **Dataset Version:** version number/date
- **Recording Quality:** sample rate, bit depth, environment
- **Language and Accent:** BCP-47 tag, region subtag
- **Transcription Quality:** accuracy percentage, method
- **Dataset Splits:** train/validation/test ratios
- **Restrictions:** any limitations on use/distribution
- **Date Added:** ISO8601
- **Last Verified:** ISO8601

### Synthetic Data (Directive Part I, Section 22)
- **Purpose:** Controlled experiments, parameter coverage, testing
- **Limitation:** Not a substitute for real human speech for learning human behaviour
- **Generation Method:** Vocalis acoustic laboratory (Stage 4)
- **Recording:** Experiment settings, waveform, spectrum, measurements
