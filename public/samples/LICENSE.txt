Provenance and licence for public/samples/
=========================================

Files:
  cmu_bdl_arctic_a0001.wav   "Author of the danger trail, Philip Ross."
  cmu_bdl_arctic_a0003.wav   "There had been no thawing spring, and the ice was deeply pitted"
  cmu_slt_arctic_a0001.wav   "Author of the danger trail, Philip Ross."
  cmu_slt_arctic_a0003.wav   "There had been no thawing spring, and the ice was deeply pitted"

These are reference recordings bundled with Vocalis so the studio can offer an
A/B comparison between the original human recording and the transformed output.
They are the sentences the unit packs were built from, not resampled copies of
the packs themselves.

Source corpus
-------------
CMU ARCTIC databases for speech synthesis research.

  Copyright (c) 2003 Carnegie Mellon University
  http://www.festvox.org/cmu_arctic/

  Speakers used here:
    bdl - a male American English speaker
    slt - a female American English speaker

Modifications made by Vocalis
-----------------------------
* The recordings are stored as 16 kHz 16-bit mono WAV files.
* The a0003 sentence is trimmed to its first clause ("There had been no thawing
  spring, and the ice was deeply pitted") so both bundled reference sentences
  are of comparable length.
* No pitch, tempo, spectral or dynamic modification has been applied to these
  files. All transformation happens at runtime on copies.

Related files
-------------
The unit packs the engine concatenates speech from live in
public/voicepacks/cmu-arctic/{bdl,slt}/ and carry the same licence; each
directory holds its own LICENSE.txt next to diphones.bin and manifest.json.
